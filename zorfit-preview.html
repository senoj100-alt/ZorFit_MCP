How it works

Three steps. Then ask anything.

No new dashboard habit. ZorFit_MCP lives behind the AI tools you already use, and serves your real numbers only when your agent asks.

Step 01

Sign in and connect sources.

Use the connections dashboard to add supported activity, nutrition, recovery, and wearable data sources.

Step 02

Paste your MCP URL.

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP-speaking agent to the live ZorFit MCP endpoint.

Step 03

Ask useful questions.

Your assistant can compare training load, workout history, nutrition, sleep, heart rate, and recovery patterns.
